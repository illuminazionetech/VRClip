<b>Download</b>
Videos and audio from the sites supported by yt-dlp, whole playlists included
A high resolution preset for 360 and 3D that picks the largest video your device can decode
Metadata, subtitles and thumbnails embedded in the files, aria2c as optional downloader
Custom yt-dlp commands with templates

<b>Watch</b>
Built-in player that recognises flat, 360, 180 and 3D video by itself
360 and 180 video panned with touch or by moving the phone
3D files shown side by side for a viewer, as anaglyph or one eye at a time
Resume, speed, audio and subtitle tracks, gestures, picture in picture

<b>2D to 3D</b>
Turns any flat video into 3D with the Depth Anything V2 depth model, on the device
Live while you watch: on a phone, tilt it to look around the scene, no glasses needed
Or converted once into a side by side 3D file

<b>Meta Quest</b>
Immersive player built on the Meta Spatial SDK: a large screen for flat video, a sphere for 360,
native stereo for 3D files and live 2D to 3D, passthrough, a control bar you can grab

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Open source under GPL-3.0.
